# Trademark policy

Boardroom's code and instructions are released under the [MIT License](LICENSE). You can use, copy, change, fork and redistribute them, including commercially, as long as you keep the license notice.

The MIT License covers copyright in the code. It does not cover the **Boardroom** name or the round-table mark (the white table with an amber seat and five grey seats). Those identify the official project maintained by Aaron Perkins at [github.com/aidoggo/boardroom](https://github.com/aidoggo/boardroom), so that people can tell the original from a fork.

## What you can do without asking

- Say that your project is "based on Boardroom," "a fork of Boardroom," or "compatible with Boardroom."
- Use the name to refer to this project accurately, in articles, talks, reviews, tutorials and comparisons.
- Keep the name and mark in an unmodified copy you redistribute, such as a mirror or a package of the official release.
- Fork the repository on GitHub. A fork keeps the name until you publish or distribute it as your own product.

## What needs a different name

If you publish a modified version as your own plugin, product or service, give it a name and logo of your own. Please don't:

- call it "Boardroom" or a confusingly similar name, such as "Boardroom Pro" or "Boardroom AI";
- use the round-table mark or a close variation of it;
- suggest that the official project made, endorses or supports it when it doesn't.

## Questions

Not sure whether your use is fine? Open an issue at [github.com/aidoggo/boardroom/issues](https://github.com/aidoggo/boardroom/issues).
