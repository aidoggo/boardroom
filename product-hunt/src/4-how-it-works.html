Boardroom BETA

From question to filed outcomes.

1

Convene

Ask for a meeting. The chair confirms the question and who attends.

2

Seat

Each agent joins with its own rules, files and voice.

3

Discuss

Members answer each other. Every turn is relayed word for word.

4

You decide

One question at a time, with each member's recommendation.

5

Close

Minutes record every decision as yours. Each member files its own outcome.

No member writes files, sends email or spends money during the meeting.
A verbatim transcript, kept outside every git repository.
No code and no network calls of its own. Just instructions.
