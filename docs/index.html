The relay problem

Stop carrying messages between your agents.

You keep a legal agent in one folder and a finance agent in another. When a decision touches both, you ask one, copy its answer to the other, and copy the reply back. Boardroom replaces that relay with a meeting.

WITHOUT BOARDROOM

You relay every answer by hand, and context gets lost in translation.

WITH BOARDROOM

Agents answer each other directly. You hear the whole room, then decide.

A meeting, start to finish

From one question to filed outcomes.

Ask Claude to convene the boardroom. The session you ask becomes a neutral chair that runs the agenda and never argues a side.

  1. Convene

    "Convene the boardroom on the reseller's contract terms." The chair confirms the question and who attends.

  2. Seat

    Each member joins as its own background agent and reads its own files before it speaks.

  3. Discuss

    Members answer each other. Every turn reaches every member word for word, so everyone hears the same room.

  4. You decide

    One question at a time, with each member's recommendation in its own words.

  5. Close

    Minutes record every decision as yours. Each member files its own outcome in its own home.

You decide

One decision at a time.

When the room needs you, the chair puts exactly one question to you, with each member's case in its own words and a neutral statement of the choice.

  • Recommendations are attributed, never paraphrased.
  • Other questions wait as a count, so you face one at a time.
  • Missing facts go to a parking lot with an owner, not to you mid-meeting.
  • You give a provisional answer, hear any concerns, then decide.

Room rules

Your agents advise. You run the company.

Boardroom is a set of instructions that Claude Code follows. These rules are written into every meeting.

Every decision is yours

Members advise. The minutes record every decision in your name.

Verbatim, always

The transcript holds every turn word for word. The chair never summarizes a member in place of its words.

Hands off during the meeting

No member writes files, sends email, books calendar events or spends money while the room is in session.

Each member files its own side

At the close, every member writes its outcome in its own home, under its own rules, and never in anyone else's.

Transcripts stay out of git

Transcripts are written with owner-only permissions in a folder outside every repository.

No code, no network calls

The plugin is plain instructions. It sends nothing to its author or any service of its own.

Your roster

Bring the agents you already have.

Any agent with a home folder can take a seat: a CLAUDE.md, a decisions file, its working records. List each one once in a roster.

No roster yet? Your first meeting walks you through setup, one question at a time.

 members.md
## finance

- **Who:** a finance agent that keeps the
  company's books and forecasts.
- **Home:** `~/agents/finance`
- **Loads:** `CLAUDE.md`, `decisions.md`,
  `references/accounts.md`.
- **Speaks on:** revenue, costs, cash,
  runway, pricing, and what a decision costs.
- **Files outcomes:** a dated entry
  in `rulings.md`.
